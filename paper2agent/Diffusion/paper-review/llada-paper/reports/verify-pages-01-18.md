## Technical question answered from the package

**Question:** What learning-rate schedule and optimiser settings were used to pre-train LLaDA 8B, and do the token budgets of the schedule phases add up to the corpus size?

**Answer using only the package** (`references/paper.md`, section "2.2 Pre-training", the paragraphs beginning "The LLaDA model is pre-trained ..." and "We adopted the Warmup-Stable-Decay [28] ..."):
- The schedule is Warmup-Stable-Decay. The learning rate rises linearly from 0 to $4\times10^{-4}$ over the first 2000 iterations and stays at $4\times10^{-4}$. After 1.2T tokens it is decayed to $1\times10^{-4}$ and held for the next 0.8T tokens. It is then reduced linearly from $1\times10^{-4}$ to $1\times10^{-5}$ over the last 0.3T tokens.
- Optimiser: AdamW with weight decay 0.1, (global) batch size 1280, local batch size 4 per GPU. Sequence length is fixed at 4096, and 1% of the data is given a random length drawn uniformly from [1, 4096]. The run was executed once, without hyperparameter tuning.
- The phases cover 1.2T + 0.8T + 0.3T = 2.3T tokens. This equals the 2.3T-token pre-training corpus, so the schedule corresponds to a single pass over 2.3T tokens. The sum is my arithmetic; the paper states the corpus size and the phase lengths separately.

**Check against the PDF:** p. 4, 220 dpi crop (`_scratch/v1/p4top.png`). Every value matches exactly: 4 × 10^-4, 2000 iterations, 1.2T, 1 × 10^-4, 0.8T, 1 × 10^-4 to 1 × 10^-5, 0.3T, 0.1, 1280, 4, 4096, 1%, [1, 4096] and "executed once". The answer is fully supported.
